The bag target and its price (revenue target ÷ bag target) cover single bags and combos. Gift bags are not in the bag target, so they are judged on their own target: the average month of the last three, at their own price. Month end = so far × the same pace as the projection cards. Effect at month end = what each group will bring in minus what its target expects. Effect on revenue rate = that amount as points of the revenue target. Combo revenue is the combo's price, shared across the bags inside it.
