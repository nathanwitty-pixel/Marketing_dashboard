On offer?

All six were genuinely on offer — real list-price cuts of KES 400–1,400 (15–39% off): Fabela −400 (-17%), Aurora −600 (-29%), Mini Zuri −1,000 (-38%), Big Man Bag −600 (-29%), Avana HB −600 (-29%), Neo Man −400 (-25%), Double Press −600 (-23%), Gym Bag −600 (-23%), Zuri −1,400 (-39%), Pioneer −400 (-15%).

Discount vs demand

Because every bag was cut, the discount and back-to-school timing can't be fully separated — both applied at once. But discount depth didn't decide the winners: the deepest cut, Zuri (-39%), sold only 2, while Fabela led with 15 on a shallower -17% cut. The cut opened the door, but demand — which bags people wanted for school — chose what actually moved.

Stock

Real inventory behind them — 2,217 still in stock (led by Fabela at 375). The offer draws down a genuine stock position, not a token one.

Season

All six sit in Backpack, Handbag, Man Bag, Sling, Sport, Travel — school categories — and the step-up landed in the offer week as schools opened. Fabela and Aurora even sold on zero marketing posts (pure demand). The timing fits the sales pattern as much as the price cut does.

Good move. All six ran a real 15–39% cut and daily sales rose +-52% during back-to-school. The catch: discount depth didn't pick the winners — the deepest cut (Zuri, -39%) sold least, while the popular school lines led. Repeat the timing; you can likely trim the deepest cuts on the slow movers without losing volume.

Staff-made combos were 72 combos moving 74 units (KES 366,195) — 19% of all combo volume — vs 10 official running combos moving 321 units (KES 1,381,989). 88 combo requests were logged this month.
