How it's measured: every post the marketing sheet records for the period × 2.25 expected sales per post = expected sales. Each bag is credited with its Odoo sales in the same window, capped at its own expected (e.g. 17 posts × 2.25 = 38.25 expected; 12 sold = 31%; 50 sold = 100%). Achieved = total credited ÷ total expected. Shop stock = bags already received at the shops (live Odoo on-hand; excludes warehouse and in-transit). On offer = the bag type is in this region’s live offers (Self-Made Combos); both segments are measured the same way.
