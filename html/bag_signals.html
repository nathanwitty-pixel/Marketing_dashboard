How to read it

Four ideas from quant finance, applied to each bag's daily Kenya sales. Hover any number in the table for what it means.

Drift + noise → TrendA day's sales = the bag's average pace + luck. A rise or fall is only real when it beats the luck: trend ≥ +2 rising, ≤ −2 falling. In between it's chance — don't react to it.
Volatility → Swing & safe stockHow much daily sales jump around. Two weeks of swing grows with √14, not 14 — that sets the stock needed to be 95 % sure of not running out, and the run-out risk.
Sharpe → ReliabilityAverage sales ÷ swing. High = a steady seller: posts and pushes convert reliably. Low = erratic: one post can't be judged on one day.
Beta → Busy-day bagsHow much a bag moves with all bags' sales. Above 1.3 it booms on busy days (paydays, weekends) — stock it before them. Posting beta (extra sales per post) appears once 6 weeks of posts are saved.
