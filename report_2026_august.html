Growth % towards achieved monthly sales: 3.74% Previous sales % achieved: 72.32% (2,881 bags)
The Bottom Line

We finished August at 76.1% of target (17,671 bags), 5,561 bags short. Weekly output averaged about 2,945 bags — under the 5,808-bag weekly floor the target requires.

The Insight

Weekly output ran below the bare minimum needed to reach target most weeks. The shortfall is one of throughput and consistency, not demand — corporate orders (65 bags) only nudged the forecast to 76.34%.

Recommendation
StartManaging to a hard 5,808-bag weekly commit, reviewed every Monday — not a single monthly number reconciled at month-end.
StopTreating the ~2,863-bag weekly gap as normal. Each missed week is unrecoverable against a fixed monthly target.
Business Impact

Closing even half the weekly gap (~1,431 bags/week) across the month adds ~5,726 bags — lifting achievement from 76.1% to ~100.7%.

Assumes ~1,431 bags/week recovered × 4 weeks; at ~KES 2,500/bag that is ≈ KES 14,314,167 of recovered sales.

The Bottom Line

Across 10 combos and 10 power deals (Kenya), the two offer types cleared stock very differently: combos moved 2,509 bags (KES 0) while power deals moved 3,745 bags (KES 2,599,700). Power deals shifted the most stock; power deals made the most money.

The Insight

It's a price-point story: combos averaged KES 0 per offer vs power deals KES 2,025. The cheaper combos clear more volume, so dead stock moves fastest through combos — yet 4,424 bags remain in Kenya offer stock (plus 488 Sinza, 754 Uganda), so the mix still isn't drawing inventory down fast enough.

Sinza & Uganda offers

Sinza ran 10 combos, 10 singles and 0 specials, moving 206 bags (value 49,500) against 488 in stock. Uganda ran 16 combos and 0 singles, moving 78 bags (value 36) against 754 in stock. Both are clearing only a thin slice of their offer stock — the same dead-stock problem as Kenya, at smaller scale.

Dead stock or marketing? Sinza moved 206 bags — 42.2% of its stock cleared, with posting sales-achieved at 20.4%. The bigger lever was the offer/price clearing dead stock. Uganda moved 78 bags — 10.3% of its stock cleared, with posting sales-achieved at 15.3%. The bigger lever was marketing posting — but both are modest, so plenty of dead stock is still stuck.

Recommendation
StartRouting the slowest dead stock through combos (the higher-volume clearer) and keeping premium movers in power deals for margin.
StopRestocking the slowest-moving colours until the 4,424-bag Kenya position draws down.
Business Impact

Clearing just 20% of the 4,424-bag Kenya position (~885 bags) in the month is ~3.8% of target — and frees working capital tied up in slow stock.

Assumes a 20% draw-down of the reported Kenya offer stock; excludes Sinza/Uganda upside.

The Insight

Where we post, we sell: monthly sales on posted bags (5,863) ran ~3.9× the 1,516 expected. Yet only 4,424 bags were on offer/posted vs 5,192 not. Regionally the discipline collapses — Kenya 85.9%, Sinza 20.4%, Uganda 15.3% — so the biggest untapped demand is in Uganda and in unmarketed stock.

Focus — move the bags NOT on offer

Marketing's real target is the stock sitting not on offer. Read across the three posting lenses:

CombinedSales vs Expected (Posted & Unposted) — of Kenya's in-stock bags, 4,424 are on offer but 5,192 sit not on offer: dead stock we aren't even putting in front of buyers.
With postingSales with Posting vs Expected — where marketing posted, sales ran ~3.9× expected. Posting demonstrably works, so aiming it at the not-on-offer pile is the lever.
No postingSales with no Posting vs Opportunity if Marketed — the sales team already moved 2,023 bags with zero marketing; the gap up to the opportunity is what marketing leaves on the table by not posting the not-on-offer stock.

Not-on-offer stock by region: Kenya 5,192, Sinza 308, Uganda 540 — and Sinza (20.4%) and Uganda (15.3%) move it far slower than Kenya (85.9%). Same playbook, all three regions.

Recommendation
StartSystematically posting the 5,192 unmarketed in-stock bags — the single largest, cheapest lever against the 5,561-bag target gap.
Start testingThe Kenya posting playbook in Uganda, where sales-achieved is 15.3% vs Kenya's 85.9%.
StopLetting in-stock bags sit unposted — every unmarketed bag is a bag that reliably does not sell.
Business Impact

If the 5,192 unposted bags converted at even a conservative 20% once marketed, that is ~1,038 bags — enough to close ~18.7% of the entire target gap. Bringing Uganda toward Kenya's rate compounds it further.
