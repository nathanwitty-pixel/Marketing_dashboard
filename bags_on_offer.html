Bags on offer vs not on offer

Money per week ON OFFERNOT ON OFFEROTHERS

On offer = combo sales + single sales of bags in a timed offer, a running combo, a Deal of the Week or a Power Deal. Not on offer = every other bag, including this month’s new products that aren’t on a deal. Others = gift bags, samples + corporate invoices.

Where the on-offer money comes from

Counted by how it was sold, each sale once: a bag printed inside a combo counts in Combo sales; a bag sold singly counts in the first of Timed offer → Power Deal → Deal of the Week → Combo bag sold singly it belongs to (a timed offer counts only at its shops, dates and hours; a Power Deal runs at every shop all month; a Deal of the Week only at its shops, in its tier’s weeks). A sale that fits none is not on offer. The rows add up to the on-offer total. Bar length is relative to the largest row; the % is the share of on-offer money.

Offer type summary

Every sale in exactly one offer type: Combos = combo sales + combo bags sold singly (a Jumbo + Jumbo is 1 combo sale holding 2 bags); Mid-month / others = timed offers + samples. Category and Tier are for the period picked at the top; a combo’s money is split evenly over its bags. Tiers come from bag_tiers.csv — bags without one sit under No tier.
