Bags on offer vs not on offer

Money per week ON OFFERNOT ON OFFEROTHERS

On offer = combo sales + single sales on a Power Deal or a Deal of the Week (a combo bag bought on its own and a timed-offer sale count as not on offer). Not on offer = every other bag, including this month’s new products that aren’t on a deal. Others = gift bags, samples + corporate invoices.

Where the on-offer money comes from

Counted by how it was sold, each sale once: a bag printed inside a combo counts in Combo sales; a bag sold singly counts in Power Deal (every shop, all month) or else Deal of the Week (only at its shops, in its tier’s weeks). Everything else is not on offer, including a combo bag bought on its own (full price) and a timed-offer sale (a short sales boost, tracked on the Timed Offers menu). The rows add up to the on-offer total. Bar length is relative to the largest row; the % is the share of on-offer money.

Combo bags: inside combos vs on their own

For every bag in a running combo: units sold inside combos (printed on the combo button, running and self-made combos, refunds taken off) against units sold on its own (every single sale: Power Deal, Deal of the Week or not on offer), for the period picked at the top. The bar shows the share inside combos; the tag says which way it sells more.

Offer type summary

Every sale in exactly one offer type: Combos (button) = combos rung on the combo button (a Jumbo + Jumbo is 1 combo sale holding 2 bags); combo bags bought on their own and timed-offer sales are Not on offer; Samples = sample units. Category and Tier are for the period picked at the top; a combo’s money is split evenly over its bags. Tiers come from bag_tiers.csv — bags without one sit under No tier.
