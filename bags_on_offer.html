Bags on offer vs not on offer

Money per week ON OFFERNOT ON OFFEROTHERS

On offer = combo sales + single sales of bags in a running combo, a Deal of the Week or a Power Deal. Not on offer = every other bag, including this month’s new products that aren’t on a deal. Others = gift bags, laptop sleeves, samples + corporate invoices.

Where the on-offer money comes from

Counted by how it was sold, each sale once: a bag printed inside a combo counts in Combo sales; a bag sold singly counts in the first of Power Deal → Deal of the Week → Combo bag sold singly it belongs to (a Power Deal runs at every shop all month; a Deal of the Week only at its shops, in its tier’s weeks). A sale that fits none is not on offer. The rows add up to the on-offer total. Bar length is relative to the largest row; the % is the share of on-offer money.

Bags not on offer GAP

Selling but on no offer — the strongest candidates to put on one. In combos = printed inside self-made combos (that money is in Combo sales). Stock & days of cover are today’s, as on the combos page.
