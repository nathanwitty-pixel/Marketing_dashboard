Performance

Menu 1

Current Performance

Are we on course for this month's bag target?

What you will see

  • Sales: bags sold this month. Shop tills (POS) plus corporate bags paid for this month, plus samples. Gift bags and rejects count; refunds are taken off.
  • Weekly sales and Remaining target (bags still needed).
  • Weekly performance: each Sunday to Saturday week's bags, including corporate bags paid that week, as a share of the target.
  • Forward projections: where the month ends at the current pace, without and with corporate bags, for bags and revenue.
  • Price per bag and On offer vs not on offer: why the revenue % and the bags % differ.
  • Corporate by client: what each corporate client agreed, paid and received.

How to read it

  • Target to beat = the month's target divided by its full weeks. Green means the week cleared it.
  • The badge on each week is its change on the week before. A grey "so far" badge means the week is still running, so it is not a real drop yet.
  • The dashed line is last month, week by week.

The weekly rows add up exactly to the Sales card.

Products

Menu 2

Product Performance

Which bags, categories and colours carry the sales, and why did they move?

What you will see

  • Bag of the week, the biggest mover, and why it moved: stock, offers, posting and bulk orders.
  • Top-selling bags by share of units or revenue, with the change in share.
  • Shop breakdown: units per shop, including a Corporate row for the Corporate app, with a Total row. Switch bulk orders on or off.
  • Categories, the top 5 categories with their best and weakest bags, and colours.

How to read it

  • The filters at the top (market, period, units or revenue) drive the whole page.
  • Every period is compared with the period before it, of the same length.
  • pp = percentage points: 10.0% to 10.1% of sales is +0.1 pp.
  • This page leaves out gift bags and samples, so its totals sit a little under Current Performance.
Menu 3

New Products

How are this month's new bags selling against their targets?

What you will see

  • Each new bag's target, sold and deficit, for Kenya and outside Kenya.
  • Weekly sales vs marketing posts, and Sales by shop: CORP is the Corporate app, ranked like a shop.
  • Runway bags: styles first created in Odoo in the last 30 days.
  • Top 10, colour movement with stock guidance, and customers waiting for a new bag that was out of stock.

How to read it

  • The list of new bags comes from the new-products file; the targets come from Odoo.
  • Marketing logs posts a week late, so This week shows 0 posts and Last week shows the real posting.
  • In Sales by shop, the colours rank the shops inside each row: gold is where that bag sells best.
Menu 4

Timed Offers

Did each short offer pay off, and would the next one?

What you will see

  • Offers compared: each offer's lift, revenue, real discount, discount given away, extra revenue per KES 1 of discount, sell-through and the dip after it.
  • Best bags across the offers: the bags that responded most and least.
  • Next offer check: pick bags, a discount and the days, and it predicts units a day and revenue, with a verdict.
  • Then each offer: per-bag sales in its window, prices, bulk orders and the posting that ran with it.

How to read it

  • Lift = units a day during the offer against the same bags in the 28 days before, walk-in only (orders of 10 or more taken out).
  • Above KES 1 of extra revenue per KES 1 of discount, the offer paid for itself.
  • The prediction is learned from only a few offers, so treat it as a guide.
Menu 5

Self-made vs Running Combos

Do customers buy the combos we run, or build their own?

What you will see

  • Running combos: combos on this month's combo list.
  • Self-made combos: bags a customer bought together that are not on the list.
  • For Kenya, Sinza and Uganda, plus the Power deals view and combo requests.

How to read it

  • A self-made pair that keeps selling is a candidate for next month's running combos (see Offer Picking).
  • Sinza and Uganda combos are worked out from their till receipts.
Menu 6

Bags On vs Off Offer

How much of the money comes from offers, and are the shops hitting their targets?

What you will see

  • Every sale sorted into one offer type: Not on offer, Power deals, Combos, Deal of the week, Corporate, Mid-month offers, Samples and Gift bags.
  • Offer mix by period and a weekly breakdown, with a last complete week vs the week before column. You can compare any two weeks.
  • Shops vs their Odoo targets, and the bags on and off offer.

How to read it

  • Switch between Revenue, Units and Mix % (share of the week).
  • Use Per day when a week is short. Week 1 had only 3 days in October.
  • Its All bags total matches the Sales card.
Menu 7

Shop Stock Levels

Does each shop hold the right stock, and what should go to it now?

What you will see

  • Stock monitor: each shop's bags as tiles, showing have, Min, Max, sells a week, send now, waiting and prepaid.
  • Stocks report by bag, and next month preparedness from the 15th.
  • Shop requests: what shops asked for against what they got, how good the asks were, and what can still be sent by month end.
  • Production: the approved production list against the shops' asks.

How to read it

  • Min = 1 week of sales, Max = 2.5 weeks. Weekly sales = the last 13 weeks, adjusted for the season and the shop's growth.
  • At or below Min, refill up to Max. Not moving = no sale at that shop in 13 weeks.
  • Prepaid bags are already sold, so they do not count as free on the shelf.
  • What can be sent is capped by what CBD and VLD hold now. The rest waits for production.
Menu 8

Offer Picking

Which combos and Power deals should run next month?

What you will see

  • A forecast of the picked combos, ranked for next month.
  • A picker: next month's combo grid, where each slot can be changed, with was and now price, cost, profit, and how many can be built from stock.
  • Power deals and seasonality.

How to read it

Change a slot and the price, profit and buildable count update straight away. A combo that cannot be built from stock is not a real option.

Menu 9

Reject Sale

What price should each reject bag sell at?

What you will see

  • The uploaded reject list (built for the Kitengela clearance), each bag put at KES 1,000 or KES 1,500, grouped by category, on a pricing board.

How to read it

A bag that costs KES 900 or less to make goes at KES 1,000; above that, KES 1,500. A bag that costs more than KES 1,500 to make is flagged below cost.

Marketing

Menu 10

Posting Yields

Is marketing posting the bags that sell?

What you will see

  • Kenya, Sinza and Uganda, weekly or monthly: posts against sales per bag (the posting yield) and how accurate the posting was.
  • Dead stock: bags in stock that were never posted.

How to read it

Posts come from marketing's own daily posting sheets, logged about a week late, so the latest week can look empty until marketing fills it in.

Menu 11

Shops Efficiency

How well does each shop turn what it is sent into sales?

What you will see

  • Per shop: bags dispatched, sold, in stock and sell-through, weekly or monthly.
  • Combos and Power deals by shop.
  • Stock record accuracy: negative-stock fixes per 1,000 bags sold.

How to read it

High dispatch with low sell-through means stock is piling up at that shop. A high fixes rate means its stock records are often wrong.

Menu 12

Footfall and Marketing

Do posts and ads bring people in, and do the shops turn them into buyers?

What you will see

Shop by shop and week by week: visitors counted at each shop at close of day, walk-in buyers, the share who bought, WhatsApp chats, posts and ad spend.

How to read it

More visitors with the same buyers means the shop is converting fewer of them. More posts or ads with no change in visitors means they are not bringing people in.

Menu 13

Shop Launch

How is the newest shop (Nyeri) doing since its first sale?

What you will see

Sales, sell-through of the opening stock, a day-by-day race against the last shop opened (Rongai), and whether marketing posted the bags it stocks.

How to read it

The race lines up day 1 of both shops, so you compare like with like.

Menu 14

Push Planner locked

What should we push this week, where, and why are some bags stuck?

What you will see

For Kenya, Sinza and Uganda: the bags to push this week and at which shops, the reason each bag is stuck, and whether last week's calls worked. Laya, the on-device decision model, gives a second opinion when this PC runs the page.

How to read it

Offer Picking plans next month. The Push Planner works inside the month, week by week. The decisions come from the dashboard's own numbers; Laya never overrides them.

Menu 15

Bag Signals locked

Is a bag's trend real, and is it about to run out?

What you will see

Per bag, over the last 91 days in Kenya: average sales a day, how much a day's sales swing, how reliable the bag is, how it reacts on busy days and to posting, its run-out risk, and what to post or restock.

How to read it

A big swing means one good day proves little. Trust a bag's trend only when it is reliable.

Intelligence

Menu 16

Monthly Report

The month in one report, with what to do next.

What you will see

A bottom line, then each area (current performance, new products, offer types, posting), each written in the same four steps: the conclusion first, the insight, a recommendation (stop, start or start testing) and the business impact in numbers.

How to read it

Read the bottom line and the recommendations first; the sections underneath back them up.

Menu 17

History

What did past months look like?

What you will see

The month-end snapshots, frozen when each month closes and kept in Supabase. Pick a month to see its report as it was.

How to read it

These numbers do not change after the month closes, so later fixes to the live pages do not rewrite them.

Words you will see

POS, tills
The shops' tills in Odoo. Every shop sale goes through one.
Corporate (Corporate app)
Sales through the Denri Corporate app. They count like a shop, by the money received in the period. Corporate clients who pay at a shop till count as that shop's sale.
Bulk order
One till line of 10 or more units. It is flagged ⚠ so one big order does not make a shop or bag look like it jumped.
Combo, running and self-made
Two or more bags sold together at one price. Running combos are on this month's combo list; self-made combos are pairs customers build themselves.
Power deal
A bag on offer for the whole month, in Kenya.
Deal of the week
A bag on offer for a week, at the shops running that deal.
Mid-month offers
New products, runway bags, timed offers, and the listed singles in Tanzania and Uganda.
Timed offer
A short campaign on chosen bags for set dates, such as Mashujaa Offers on 19 to 24 Oct.
Runway bags
Styles first created in Odoo in the last 30 days.
Gift bags, samples
Gift bags are the small gift bags (A3, A4 and colours) sold at the till. Samples are display units sold off. Both count in Sales.
Refunds, combo refunds
Refunds are taken off sales once. A refunded combo takes off each of its bags exactly once.
Prepaid
A customer paid for a bag the shop did not have and is waiting for it. Those bags are already sold.
Waiting
A customer asked for a bag that was out of stock (on WhatsApp or in the shop) and has not bought since.
Min, Max, send now
Min is 1 week of a shop's sales and Max is 2.5 weeks. At or below Min, send enough to reach Max.
Weekly sales rate
The last 13 weeks of sales, adjusted for the season (the same weeks in 2024 and 2025) and the shop's growth over the last year.
Target, need
The month's bag targets from Odoo, split per shop and per bag. Need is a shop's share of a bag's target.
Week, partial week
Weeks run Sunday to Saturday, cut at the month's edges. A week still running reads "so far" and is not a real drop.
Lift
How much faster a bag sold during an offer than before it.
Sell-through
The share of the stock a shop held that it sold.
Posting yield
How well posts line up with what sells. Posts are logged about a week late.
Kenya, Sinza, Uganda
The three markets. Sinza covers Tanzania's two tills (Sinza and Dar es Salaam). Their money is shown in KES at 25 TSh and 29 USh to the shilling.
CBD, VLD
The two stores that restock the Kenya shops. Sinza and Uganda have their own stores.
Ads impact
A panel built from marketing's weekly ads report. It is hidden on every page for now.
